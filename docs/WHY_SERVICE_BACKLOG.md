# Why `ServiceBacklogWorld` exists (Phase M)

This repository’s first domains were a **peg**, a **contagion network**, and a **resource cascade** — all still useful, but all still **crypto-shaped** stories. Phase **M** adds a **third reference domain** that is deliberately **operations-shaped**: a work **backlog** and **service slack** stressed by the **same** abstract shock vocabulary (`reserve_loss`, `rumor`) and the **same** schedule encoding as the other worlds.

## What we use it for

- **Engine parity:** replay JSON, Phase **H** benchmarks, co-evolution, Pareto export, Monte Carlo, counterfactuals, and ε-sweeps — without claiming this toy matches a real ticketing system.
- **Teaching separation:** shocks hit **load** vs **capacity morale / staffing / trust** (slack) differently; collapse is **queue blow-up** or **slack floor**, not a de-peg.

## What we do **not** claim

- No fit to production incident data, SLA contracts, or staffing models.
- No assertion that “price” in replay JSON is a market price — here `metrics.price` is **slack headroom** for viewer compatibility with the shared schema.
